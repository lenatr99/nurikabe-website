Nurikabe

Logic Islands

A relaxing Japanese number-logic puzzle. Build white islands, fill the seas, and find the one true solution.

How to Play

  • 1
    Every number is an island. Each island contains exactly that many cells.
  • 2
    Islands cannot touch each other (except diagonally).
  • 3
    All sea cells must connect into one continuous area.
  • 4
    No 2×2 block of sea is allowed.
